Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Falkryn Eyas ("OnBase," "we," "us," or "our") built OnBase, a voice-first dating app for the active duty military, veteran, DoD/federal, and federal first responder community. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using OnBase, you agree to the practices described here.

🛡️
Who OnBase is for.

OnBase is restricted to adults 21 and older who are active duty military, veterans, DoD/federal employees, or federal first responders. Eligibility is verified through our identity-verification partner, ID.me, before an account can be used. OnBase is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 21.

Information we collect

CategoryWhat it includes
Account & profileName, email address, phone number, and profile details you choose to add.
Eligibility verificationConfirmation of your military, veteran, DoD/federal, or first-responder affiliation, provided to us by ID.me. We do not receive or store the underlying identity documents you submit to ID.me.
Voice & mediaVoice profile recordings, and any photos or videos you choose to share as part of the voice-first reveal process.
LocationApproximate or precise location, used only to support mutual, consent-based in-person and virtual date check-ins.
MessagesCommunications exchanged with matches inside the app.
Device & diagnostic dataStandard technical information (such as device type and crash/error logs) used to keep the app secure, stable, and working correctly.

How we use this information

  • App functionality — creating and authenticating your account, verifying eligibility, operating the voice-first matching and pod system, preventing fraud and abuse, maintaining security, and providing customer support.
  • Personalization — your voice profile and, once mutually unlocked, photos and video are used to power match and pod recommendations.
  • Safety — location check-ins, reporting tools, and account verification exist to support a safer dating experience for our community.

We do not currently use your information for advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics or ad-tracking tools. If that changes, we will update this policy and the relevant app store disclosures before doing so.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising purposes. We share information only as needed to operate OnBase:

  • Other members — profile details, and voice/photo/video content, are shared with matches according to OnBase's reveal rules (voice first; photos and video only after mutual consent).
  • Service providers — we use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage, Cloudinary for media hosting, and ID.me for eligibility verification. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • Legal & safety — we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our members or the public.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain it for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Your choices & rights

  • Access, correct, or update your profile information at any time within the app.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us below.
  • Control location sharing and notification preferences within the app's settings.

Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access-controlled backend rules, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data using practices appropriate to its sensitivity.

Children's privacy

OnBase is restricted to adults 21 and older and is not directed at anyone under that age. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you through the app or by updating the effective date above.

Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns about this policy or your data can be sent to falkryneyas@gmail.com. See also our Support page.
