Common questions

How do I verify my eligibility?

OnBase is restricted to active duty military, veterans, DoD/federal employees, and federal first responders, 21 and older. During sign-up, you'll verify your affiliation through our verification partner, ID.me. If verification fails or you run into an error, email us with the address you used to sign up and we'll help sort it out.

How do I report a safety concern or inappropriate behavior?

Your safety comes first. If you experience harassment, inappropriate behavior, or anything that concerns you, use the in-app report option on the member's profile or conversation, or email us directly at falkryneyas@gmail.com with details. Reports are reviewed promptly.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Email us at falkryneyas@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and request deletion. We'll confirm once it's processed. See our Privacy Policy for details on how we handle your data.

I'm having a technical issue with the app.

Let us know what device and app version you're using and describe what happened (a screenshot helps) — email falkryneyas@gmail.com and we'll take a look.
